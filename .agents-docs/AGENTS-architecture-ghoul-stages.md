| `public/activities/ghoul-rush/stages/graveyard/ghoul-graveyard.js` | Ghoul Rush's Graveyard stage pack, its rules (no imports: `ghoul.js` hands each rule a kit `k` of its own helpers). A stage pack is plain data and functions that `ghoul.js` merges into its own tables (`PACKS`): `monsters` (with `perch`: its spawn names its height, `flies`: a horde one comes in high), `walkers`/`zombies`/`stackers`/`climbers` (joining the sets of those names), `act` (start), `solid`, `move`, `kill`, `horde` (fields a horde one comes with), `bosses`, `boss` (one slice of its fight), `guard` (true while shots and stomps glance off, a `bossBlock` event) and `hazards` (by kind, one slice of a box on the floor that hurts while `live`; kept in `round.hazards`). Three more fields a stage may add (the Grounds does): `cut` (kinds the Hedge shears kill outright), `weak` (by boss kind, `(boss, shot) => damage multiplier` for a weak spot; `bossHit` then carries `how: "weak"` and `weak: true`) and `cause` (what a boss's touch is called when it costs a life). The Chapel adds more: `tick(round, ms, k)` (a stage-wide twist run first in every slice; it may set `round.stunMs`, which freezes you and every monster, boss, hazard and missile while bolts in flight still fly, and keeps its state in `round.twist`), `stone` (by kind, true while shots glance off and a stomp does nothing; `glance` event), `shot(round, m, s, k, random)` (the damage multiplier of a bolt that hit `m`, free to splash), `target(round, s, k)` (true when a bolt struck something of the pack's that is no monster, such as an organ pipe) and, on a monster, `hasteMs` and `haste` (its time runs faster while `hasteMs` is over 0). On the drawing side a look may be `custom: true` with a `pack` id (the pack's art then draws the whole wall, floor and shelves) and the art may return `decor` (by the look's `decor` name). On the drawing side a stage's art may also return `react(e, k)` (its own events; `ghoul-draw.js`'s `react` falls through to `ART.react`, which returns true when it handled `e`), `ground` and `shelf` (the floor pieces and shelves of an outdoor look), `waves` and `powers`; a boss event must carry `kind` or its voice never plays. The Graveyard's: grasping hands (`HANDS_*`: lurk unseen, rise, grab, sink, creep under level ground after you), the mourner (wails every `WAIL_MS`, slowing you to `SLOW_SHARE` speed for `SLOW_MS` within `WAIL_REACH`; `round.you.slowMs`), the grave robber (digs in within `ROBBER_DIG`, comes up `ROBBER_BEHIND` behind you), the rotter (a zombie whose body-shot death leaves its `legs` walking), the carrion crow (perches, dives at your chest, climbs, dives again), and Mother Mourning (`angel`: weeps `ANGEL_WEEP_MS` as stone flinging headstones, turns `ANGEL_TURN_MS`, watches `ANGEL_WATCH_MS` when only she can be hurt and any move drops a `fist` hazard after `FIST_WARN_MS`; at `ANGEL_RAGE` her wings spread, the weep shorter, the watch longer, a shockwave each way as each weep begins) |
