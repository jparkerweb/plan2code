    Theme
    Card width

    How wide the question and documents are. On a smaller window they fit the space there is.

    Highlight color

    Your highlight color marks what is yours to do, and Planny's star uses it while he works. Green, amber and red also mean done, staged and careful, so a highlight close to one of those can blur which is which.

    Sounds

    Ticking one plays it. Play sounds switches them all off at once and keeps your picks for when you switch back on.

    Cleanup

    Finds what Plan2Code doesn't need in your ~/.plan2code folder: sessions nobody has opened for 30 days, and files or folders that ended up there by mistake. You see the list before anything is deleted. Your preferences, saved folders, models, the launcher and this session always stay.

    Models

    You usually don't need to change this. Plan2Code ships an up-to-date model list with every release. Add a model here only when a new one comes out before Plan2Code has been updated.

    • Restart plan2code to see a change in its model menu.
    • At any time, run plan2code --model <id>, or pick your model inside your agent before running a skill.
    • Updating Plan2Code refreshes the built-in list. Models you added stay, unless the built-in list now includes them.
    • Devin models are limited to Low, Medium and High effort.
